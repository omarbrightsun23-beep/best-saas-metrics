Quantitative Valuation & Unit Economics Formulas

CAC Payback Period Formula: CAC Payback (Months) = Fully Loaded CAC / (Monthly ARPA × Gross Margin %). Measuring payback without adjusting for gross margin overstates capital efficiency by 20% to 40%.

LTV:CAC Ratio: LTV:CAC = ((Monthly ARPA × Gross Margin %) / Monthly Churn Rate) / Fully Loaded CAC. A healthy SaaS benchmark is 3.0x to 5.0x+.
